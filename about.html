The mission

Tradition meets modern training

Wariors Martial Arts Club is a martial arts and fitness club in Emirates City, Ajman, for kids, teens and adults of every level.

Our mission

Train hard. Live with purpose.

To provide authentic martial arts training rooted in tradition and enhanced by modern fitness. We build strength, discipline and confidence through expert-led programs that develop physical ability, mental focus and self-belief.

Our vision

To become a premier hub for martial arts excellence that unites tradition and modern training: an inclusive community built on discipline, confidence and respect, where people of all ages grow in strength, resilience and inner balance.

What we do

Four pillars

Every member trains across these, whatever program they choose.

Martial arts training

Karate, Kalaripayattu, Kickboxing, Boxing, MMA and Muay Thai, taught with technique and respect first.

Strength & conditioning

Power, stamina and endurance work that makes you a better athlete in every discipline.

Movement & fitness

Gymnastics floor, CrossFit, fitness and EMS for mobility, control and performance.

Mind & body wellness

Yoga, Zumba and aerobics for flexibility, rhythm, focus and calm.

Your free trial

Train freely. Move with purpose.

The trial is your introduction to the Wariors style: martial arts values combined with modern fitness to build discipline, strength, balance and focus.

  • All levels welcome. No experience needed.
  • Wear sportswear and bring your gear if you have it.
  • Arrive 10–15 minutes early for a short introduction.
  • Secure digital lockers and a filtered water station. Bring your own bottle.
  • Coaches guide you and help you set your goals.
