FAQ

Before your first class

Do I need any experience?

No. All levels are welcome and coaches guide beginners step by step from the first class.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, a water bottle (we have a filtered water station) and your own gear if you have it. Arrive 10–15 minutes early for a short introduction.

From what age can children join?

We run structured classes for kids, teens and adults. Tell us your child's age in the form and we'll suggest the right class.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. The trial class is free and there's no obligation to join.

Can I try more than one program?

Yes. Many members mix martial arts with fitness or yoga. Let us know what interests you.
